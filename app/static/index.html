How to use this

1. Drop your logos in

Any number at once. Each one is traced seven different ways, because no single method wins on every image — a method that nails a solid badge will destroy fine linework, and the other way round.

2. Click every result that would actually ship

Not the best one — all of the good ones. Most logos have several usable results, and clicking all of them is what lets the app learn which methods are genuinely reliable. Clicking only one throws that away.

3. Star the one to keep

The star marks the single result written to your folder. The top-ranked result is starred for you as a starting guess; it is the right choice about 8 times in 10, so change it whenever your eye disagrees. Your eye wins.

If none of them are any good

Press Nothing usable. That is a real answer and it is worth recording — being forced to star something you would never send to a customer is worse than saving nothing. Roughly 1 logo in 14 beats every method; those are the ones worth cleaning up first (crop tight, raise contrast, flatten to solid black on white) or redrawing by hand.

Saving

Choose a folder in Save to at the top, then press Save favourites. Every starred result is written there as an SVG named after the original image. The folder is remembered for next time.

Getting a different one later

Nothing is thrown away. History reopens any past run, so if the file you saved turns out wrong on the machine you can come back and grab a different method without re-tracing.
